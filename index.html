📜 Community Rules & Safety

These rules help keep Chat Me welcoming, useful and safer for everyone. They apply to posts, comments, messages, groups and chatrooms.

1. Be respectful

Do not harass, threaten, bully, or deliberately target other members. Disagreements are okay; personal attacks are not.

2. No spam or abuse

Do not flood chats, repeatedly post unwanted promotions, manipulate engagement, or use automated activity to disrupt the community.

3. Protect privacy

Do not share another person's private information without permission. Respect requests to remove personal information from posts or messages.

4. Be authentic

Do not impersonate another person, staff member, or organization. Do not use deceptive accounts to mislead people.

5. Share appropriate content

Keep content suitable for a general community environment and follow applicable laws and platform safety requirements.

6. Use reporting tools

If something breaks these rules, use Chat Me's reporting or customer-service tools rather than escalating the conflict yourself.

7. Keep your account secure

Use your own account, keep your login information private, and contact support if you believe your account has been compromised.

8. Moderation

Staff may review reports and take proportionate action on content or accounts that violate the rules. Staff access is controlled by trusted backend roles.

9. Featured posts

Staff may feature an important community announcement at the top of Home. A featured post is a visibility feature, not an endorsement of a member's personal views.

10. Account safety

Use your own account and keep passwords and verification codes private. Do not attempt to access another person's account or bypass security controls.

11. Content ownership and sharing

Only upload or share content you have the right to use. Do not copy, distribute, or repost another person's private material without permission.

12. Friends, messages and groups

Friend requests, private messages, groups and chatrooms must not be used for harassment, fraud, spam, unwanted bulk promotion, or attempts to collect passwords, codes or sensitive information.

13. Reporting and enforcement

Use Report or Customer Service for problems. Do not abuse the reporting system with knowingly false reports. Staff may remove content or restrict accounts when necessary to enforce these rules and protect the community.

14. Changes

These rules may be updated as Chat Me grows. The latest version shown here applies to use of the service.

Getting started

Profile & settings

Use Settings & Privacy to manage available profile, privacy, notification and messaging preferences. Keep your account credentials private.

Posts & media

Create posts, add supported media where available, react, comment, save posts and use the available sharing features. You remain responsible for content you submit.

Friends & messages

Send and manage friend requests, communicate through private messages, and participate in groups and chatrooms. Respect other members and their privacy.

Reporting

Use the Report button or Customer Service when you see rule-breaking content, account problems, scams, abuse or technical issues. Staff can review reports and take action under the Community Rules.

Account deletion

If an account-deletion control is available in Settings, read its confirmation carefully before using it because deletion may be permanent.

Need more help?

Use Customer Service to create a support ticket. For privacy requests, use the privacy contact listed in the Privacy Policy.
